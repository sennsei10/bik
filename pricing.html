Where your money goes

No middlemen taking the biggest slice

We built Petsama because we were the ones doing the actual work and keeping the smallest share of it. So we flipped it.

85%
10%
5%
85% — your sitter. The person actually showing up and caring for your pet.
10% — community ops. Background checks, sitter support, dispute handling, insurance buffer.
5% — payments & upkeep. Gateway fees and keeping the platform running.

Why we care about this

We're a small team of pet sitters who've spent years doing this work for other platforms — and watched a big chunk of every booking disappear before it reached us. Petsama isn't a company hiring sitters. It's sitters who decided to run this ourselves, so more of what you pay reaches the person your pet actually spends time with. We're not trying to be the cheapest option in Bengaluru. We're trying to be the fairest one — for you and for the sitter at your door.

Questions people ask

Why aren't you the cheapest option in the city?

We could be — by paying sitters less. We'd rather keep sitter pay fair and be transparent about it than win on a race-to-the-bottom price that quietly comes out of someone's earnings.

Are there any extra charges later?

No booking fees, no "convenience" charges, no surprise add-ons. The price you see is confirmed by your sitter before the visit and that's what you pay.

What if my pet needs something outside a standard visit?

Message us on WhatsApp — medication, multiple pets, extra playtime, whatever it is, we'll quote it upfront before anything is booked.

How do I pay?

UPI or card, confirmed directly with your sitter once the booking is set. Payment only happens after your details are confirmed — nothing charged upfront just for filling the form.
